Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ build_date | default('June 5, 2026') }}

This Privacy Policy describes how {{ site_title }} (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit {{ site_url }}/.

1. Information We Collect

We collect a minimal amount of information necessary to operate the Service and improve user experience:

  • Usage data: pages viewed, time spent, referring source, device type, and country (via Google Analytics 4 — anonymized IP addresses).
  • Voluntary submissions: if you subscribe to our Telegram bot, we store the chat ID and your category/time preferences solely to deliver digests.
  • Cookies: essential cookies for site functionality; analytics cookies via Google Analytics 4 (you may opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on).

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To analyze aggregate usage trends (no individual identification).
  • To deliver subscribed news digests via Telegram.
  • To respond to inquiries or support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information as governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — web analytics (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdSense — advertising (if enabled on this site; uses cookies for ad personalization — you can opt out via Google Ads Settings)
  • Telegram Bot API — for subscription delivery (Telegram Privacy Policy)

Articles on this site contain links to external news sources. Clicking those links will take you to a third-party website whose privacy practices we do not control.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share anonymized, aggregate statistics (e.g., "X users from Y country visited this article") with our news source partners and the public.

5. AI Training

Our site is configured to opt out of AI training crawlers via the robots.txt file. The following crawlers are explicitly disallowed: GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Bytespider, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, and others listed in our robots.txt.

6. Data Retention

  • Google Analytics: data is retained for 14 months (Google's default), then automatically deleted.
  • Telegram subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe via the bot's /stop command.
  • Server logs: retained for 30 days for security and debugging, then deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. To exercise these rights, contact us through the channels listed on our homepage. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at the contact information provided on our homepage.
